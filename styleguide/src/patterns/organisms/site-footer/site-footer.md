---
title: Site footer
---

The bottom of every page, one row: the line in `storefront.site.footer` on the left, and on the right the links to
all pages (the home page's map) and to the search. The frame (`pages/frame`) keeps it at the bottom of the viewport
on a short page, and on a wide screen with room to spare (64em wide, 40em high) it stays there while the page
scrolls.
