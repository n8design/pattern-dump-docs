---
title: Pager
---

The previous and the next page, in the navigation's order, at the end of an article: `page.prev` and `page.next`,
derived by Pattern Dump. A layout that declares both includes it, `{{> molecules-pager }}`. The first page has no
previous one and the last no next; a page with neither has no pager.

Previous is outlined, next is filled in heading ink, each with a small label and the page's title.
